# Global Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Global Superstore sales and profitability data using Microsoft Excel. It explores sales performance, profit trends, product categories, regional markets, customer segments, discounts, and returns to identify business patterns and opportunities for improvement.

## Business Objectives

Analyze sales and profit trends over time.

Compare performance across regions and customer segments.

Identify product categories and subcategories with strong or weak profitability.

Explore the relationship between discounts and profit.

Examine returned orders and their potential impact on business performance.

Support business decisions through structured analysis and reporting.

## Tools & Excel Techniques

**Microsoft Excel** – data analysis and reporting.

**Pivot Tables** – summarizing sales and profit by different dimensions.

**XLOOKUP** – retrieving related information across datasets.

**Profitability Calculations** – evaluating sales and profit performance.

**Charts and Dashboards** – presenting business metrics visually.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Sales Performance

Explore sales trends and compare performance across time periods, regions, and customer segments.

### 2. Product Profitability

Analyze profit across product categories and subcategories to identify stronger and weaker performers.

### 3. Discount Analysis

Explore how discount levels relate to sales and profitability.

### 4. Regional and Customer Analysis

Compare business performance across geographic markets and customer segments.

### 5. Returns Analysis

Examine returned orders and consider their implications for sales performance and profitability.

## Key Findings

The project investigates differences in sales and profitability across products, regions, customer segments, discounts, and returns.

Specific findings should be documented using the actual results from the Excel analysis and dashboard.

## Business Recommendations

Review low-profit and loss-making product categories to identify opportunities for improvement.

Evaluate discount strategies in relation to their impact on profitability.

Compare regional performance to identify markets that may benefit from targeted actions.

Monitor returns alongside sales and profit metrics.

Use recurring sales and profitability reports to support business planning.

These recommendations should be validated against the numerical results of the analysis before being used for business decisions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Excel for business data analysis, profitability assessment, data summarization, and reporting. It highlights how spreadsheet tools can help transform business data into insights that support decision-making.
